Safety Tips for Using Camphor at Home

Camphor has been a cherished part of Indian households for generations. Whether used during daily pooja, temple rituals, meditation, or simply to create a refreshing atmosphere, its soothing fragrance and spiritual significance make it a household essential. However, using camphor safely is just as important as choosing a high-quality product.

Simple Safety Tips for Using Camphor

  • Burn Camphor in a Heat-Resistant Holder — Always use a sturdy, heat-resistant holder when burning camphor. Keep it away from flammable items such as curtains, paper, wooden décor, or fabrics to reduce the risk of fire.
  • Ensure Proper Ventilation — When using camphor indoors, make sure the room is well-ventilated. Fresh airflow helps maintain a pleasant fragrance and creates a comfortable environment.
  • Store It Safely — Keep camphor in a cool, dry place away from moisture. Always store it out of reach of children and pets to prevent accidental contact or ingestion.
  • Never Leave Burning Camphor Unattended — Whether you're performing pooja or meditating, never leave burning camphor unattended. Extinguish it completely before leaving the room.
  • Choose Pure, High-Quality Camphor — For the best experience, choose pure Bhimseni camphor from a trusted manufacturer. High-quality camphor burns cleaner, offers a more pleasant aroma, and is ideal for spiritual and household use.
  • Use the Right Amount — A small quantity of camphor is enough to freshen your space. Using too much can make the fragrance overpowering, so use it in moderation.
